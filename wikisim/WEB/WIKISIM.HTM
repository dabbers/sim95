About WikiSim 96

WikiSim is an encyclopedia on your computer. More than a thousand articles on history, science, geography, the arts, famous people and, of course, computers and the Information Superhighway. It is a free reference for homework, research and settling arguments, and every article links to others.

Where do the articles come from?

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia: its list of the thousand most vital articles, and a hundred and fifty more on computers, the Internet and pop culture. Wikipedia is written by its readers, and anybody may copy it as long as they say where it came from and share it on the same terms (the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence).

Why "as of 1996"?

Because it is 1996. Every sentence that mentions a later year, or something that had not been invented yet, has been taken out, along with articles about things that had not happened. What is left is the first few sentences of each article, edited to fit on one disk. So WikiSim knows about Netscape Navigator, the Pentium and The X-Files, and is none the wiser about anything since.

How do I use it?

  • Find: type a word in the box at the top of any page.
  • A to Z: click a letter to see every title that starts with it.
  • Random article: open the encyclopedia at any page.
  • Article of the Day and Did you know? change every day, on the front page.
