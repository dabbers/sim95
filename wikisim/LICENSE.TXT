WikiSim's articles (wikisim/DATA/*.TXT, and the copies of them that
INSTALL.SPK writes to C:\WIKIDATA)
======================================================================

The articles are from Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
(https://en.wikipedia.org/), written by Wikipedia's contributors. Each
one is named after the Wikipedia article it came from, and its full text,
history and list of authors are at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<title>.

They are used, and shared, under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The text has been modified. tools/make-wikisim.mjs took the introduction
of each article and:

  * removed every sentence that mentions a year after 1996, says "as of
    20xx", or names something from after 1996 (the list is in the
    script), and left out articles about things from after 1996;
  * removed pronunciation guides and empty brackets, and tidied spacing;
  * changed "is currently" to "is at present" and "is the current" to
    "is the present";
  * shortened each article to its first few sentences;
  * added links between WikiSim's own articles, and "See also" lists.

These modified articles are likewise licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. You may
copy, change and share them for any purpose, as long as you credit
Wikipedia, say what you changed, and share the result under the same
licence.

The WikiSim program itself (wikisim/WEB, the installer and the scripts)
is part of sim95 and is not covered by this file.
