About SimPal

SimPal is the easy, secure way to send money and pay online on the SIM95 network. It is a wallet for your SimBucks (§): sign up, and you can pay anybody else with a SimPal account in seconds. Sign up today and get §100.00 FREE!

What you can do with SimPal

  • Send money to friends, family or that guy who sold you a modem, with a note.
  • Request money from people who owe you. They pay or decline with one click.
  • Shop online at any web site with a Pay with SimPal button, like SimMart. The shop never sees your password.
  • Accept payments on your own home page with a button you copy and paste, and check every receipt before you ship.
  • See your history: every payment, purchase and sale, with receipt numbers.

Is my money safe?

Your money is safe with us!! Every SimBuck is written in the ledger before it moves, so a crash can't make money or lose it, and nobody can spend more than they have. Our Secure transaction padlock is drawn in genuine text art.

(Honestly: SimBucks are play money, SimPal is a toy, there is no encryption on SIM95, and whoever runs this machine can print as much as they like. See SimPal for your web site for the fine print.)
