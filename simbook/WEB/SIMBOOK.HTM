About SimBook

SimBook is the social network for the SIM95 network. It is an online directory of people, a place to keep up with your friends, and a feed of what everybody is up to. Think of it as your own little corner of the information superhighway, with your name on it.

What you can do on SimBook

  • Make a profile with your picture, where you live, what you like and an About Me.
  • Add friends and see who their friends are. Find classmates and co-workers with People search.
  • Post status updates to your news feed, and read your friends' posts as they happen.
  • Write on Walls, reply to posts, and give them a thumbs up or a thumbs down.
  • Share photos in photo albums.
  • Poke people. Nobody knows what a poke means. That is the point.
  • Notifications tell you when somebody writes on your Wall, replies or pokes you.

Is it like Facebook, MySpace or Twitter?

A bit like all of them, years early. SimBook is a social networking site, a microblog for short status updates, and a photo sharing site, written in SPARK and running on one SIM95 machine. Everything is kept in plain text files in C:\BOOKDATA. There is no algorithm: your feed is just your friends, newest first.

Join SimBook or log in. It's free and anyone can join.
