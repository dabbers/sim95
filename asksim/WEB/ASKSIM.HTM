About AskSim

AskSim is the search engine for the SIM95 network. Ask Simms a question in plain English, like "Where can I buy a modem?", and he brings you the pages that answer it.

  • A real web crawler (a spider) visits every machine on the network once an hour and reads its pages.
  • Relevance ranking: pages that use your words most, and in their titles, come first.
  • Cached copies: when a site goes away, AskSim still has the page it last saw.
  • site:machine searches one machine. Just take me there goes straight to the best answer.
  • Webmasters: add your site, and use <meta name="keywords"> and <meta name="description"> to tell Simms what your pages are about. A robots.txt keeps him out.
