About GeoSimies

GeoSimies is free web hosting for the SIM95 network. Everybody gets a free homepage: a little piece of the World Wide Web with your name on it. No HTML? No problem. Start from a template, change the words, and you're on the Web.

What you get, free

  • A homepage in a neighborhood, with an address of your own, like Area95 1001. Live with people who like the same things you do: Area95 (UFOs and sci-fi), Simwood (movies and TV), ChipVale (computers), Hometown (family and pets), NeoTokyo (anime and games), RockCity (music), Stadium (sports) and Toyland (kids).
  • A page editor right in your browser, with a preview. Up to six pages.
  • Templates bursting with style: starry skies, marquees, blinking text and a spinning globe.
  • Free clip art: under construction signs, e-mail me buttons, rainbow lines and more. Add your own pictures too.
  • A hit counter so you know how many people stopped by.
  • A guestbook for your visitors to sign.
  • Webrings: join a ring of homepages about the same thing, or start your own.
  • Cool Site of the Day: maybe it'll be yours!

Is it like GeoCities, Tripod or Angelfire?

Very much so. GeoSimies is a homesteading community for personal home pages, written in SPARK and running on one SIM95 machine. Your pages are kept in plain text files in C:\GEODATA, and the search engines find them through the neighborhood directory.
