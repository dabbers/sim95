Your Privacy and SimStats

Hey there, surfer! You're probably here because a page you like has a SimStats tag on it. We believe the Information Superhighway works best when everybody knows the rules of the road. So here, in plain English and with no lawyers involved, is exactly what we keep.

What we write down when you visit a page that uses SimStats

  • The time of your visit.
  • Your machine's network address (like 65.16.0.3). Every machine on the SIM95 network tells every web server it talks to who it is. That's how the network works, and we can't help seeing it.
  • Your browser and its version (Voyager 1.1, Simxplorer 3.02...), which your browser announces to every web server it visits.
  • Which page you looked at, and in Simxplorer its title and the page you came from (the "referrer"). Voyager doesn't tell anybody where you came from, so with Voyager we don't know.
  • A visitor number in a cookie from this machine, so we can count you once a day instead of once a page. It's a random number. It isn't your name, and we don't have your name.

What we do NOT do

  • We don't know who you are. No names, no e-mail addresses, nothing you typed anywhere.
  • We don't sell anything to anybody. There's nobody to sell it to anyway.
  • We don't read your other cookies, your files, or anything else on your machine.

Who sees it

The owner of the site you visited sees its statistics, including the addresses of the machines that visited and the last 20 visits as they happen. Some sites make their statistics public: the public page shows the numbers, pages, browsers and referrers, but never anybody's address. The person who runs this machine can see every site's totals.

How long we keep it

The visit-by-visit log (with addresses) is kept for 14 days, then thrown away. The daily totals (how many hits, which pages, which browsers, and which machines visited how often) are kept for 100 days, then they go too. Each site's Live box keeps its last 20 visits (with addresses) until newer ones push them out. A site's all-time hit count is kept for as long as the site is here. When a site's owner deletes it, every number for it goes at once.

Don't want to be counted?

No problem, and no hard feelings. Go to Don't count me and press the button. SimStats then counts nothing about you on any site, for as long as your browser keeps our cookie.
