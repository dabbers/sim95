About SimStats

SimStats is free web statistics for the SIM95 network. How many people visit your site? Which pages do they like? Where do they come from, and what browser do they use? Paste one line of HTML into your pages, and SimStats tells you, day by day and live as it happens. It's web analytics for the rest of us: think Hitbox, think Urchin, think the Google Analytics of 1998.

What you get

  • Hits and unique visitors, today, for the last 7 days or the last 30, as a bar chart.
  • Top pages, with their titles.
  • Top referrers: the pages your visitors clicked through from.
  • Browsers: Voyager or Simxplorer, and which version.
  • Hours of the day your visitors surf at.
  • Visiting machines: which machines on the network your visitors used.
  • Live: the last 20 hits, updating by themselves in Simxplorer.
  • A hit counter for your page, 88x31 in glowing LED digits.
  • Public stats, if you want to show off.

How it works

Sign up, add your site, and SimStats gives you its tracking tag: a tiny picture that lives on this machine. Put it in your pages, on any machine. Every time somebody's browser shows the picture, SimStats counts a hit. Three flavours:

  1. The plain tag, an invisible <img>, one pixel big. Works in every browser and on GeoSimies homepages.
  2. The JavaScript tag for Simxplorer, which also sends the page's title and where the visitor came from, with the plain tag in <noscript> for Voyager.
  3. The hit counter: like the plain tag, but you can see it.

Unique visitors are counted with a cookie from this machine, which both Voyager and Simxplorer send along with the picture even when it's on somebody else's page. A browser that won't keep cookies is counted once a day per machine.
