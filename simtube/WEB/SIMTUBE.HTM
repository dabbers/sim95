About SimTube

SimTube is the movie site for the SIM95 network. Watch the latest cartoons, put up your own, give them stars and say what you think. It is free and anyone can join.

Watching

SimTube movies play in SimPlayer, the movie player for SIM95 (C:\PROGRAMS\PLAYER.SPK). Each movie's page has its address, like http://machine/tube/movie.asp?v=1. Paste it into SimPlayer's Address box and press Enter. SimPlayer shows Buffering... while the first part arrives, then starts playing while the rest download. It has Play/Pause, Stop, Rewind, a frame at a time (the < and > buttons), a seek bar you can click or drag, Loop, and Mute (yes, SimMovies beep).

No SimPlayer on your machine? Get SimPlayer and save it as C:\PROGRAMS\PLAYER.SPK.

Making a movie

A SimMovie (.SMV) is a text file you can write in Notes or make with a SPARK program. It starts with a header, then a line holding just a full stop, then one line for each frame:

SIMMOVIE 1
title=Ball
author=ann
width=160
height=120
fps=10
frames=3
bg=0
thumb=1
.
*C12;D20,60,10
C0;F10,50,21,21;C12;D30,60,10
C0;F20,50,21,21;C12;D40,60,10;S440,50

Header: title, author, width and height (up to 320 by 240), fps (frames a second, 1 to 30), frames, bg (the background colour), thumb (which frame is the movie's picture; the first, if not given) and about.

A frame is drawing commands separated by semicolons. A frame that starts with * is a keyframe: it starts from a blank screen, and SimPlayer can jump straight to it. Any other frame draws over the one before, so only what moves need be drawn again. A frame of just - changes nothing.

Cndrawing colour, 0 to 15
Bnbackground colour (for X)
Xclear the screen
Fx,y,w,hfilled box
Rx,y,w,hbox
Dx,y,rfilled circle
Ox,y,rcircle
Lx1,y1,x2,y2,...lines joining the points
Px1,y1,x2,y2,...polygon
Qx1,y1,x2,y2,...filled polygon
Tx,y,wordstext (no semicolons in it)
Ix,y,namea picture
Sfreq,msa beep (unless SimPlayer is muted)

A picture is defined on a line of its own, before it is used, with the rows of a Sketch .PIC joined by |: !ball|SIM95PIC 4 2|0CC0|C00C. Colours: 0 black, 1 maroon, 2 green, 3 olive, 4 navy, 5 purple, 6 teal, 7 silver, 8 gray, 9 red, 10 lime, 11 yellow, 12 blue, 13 magenta, 14 cyan, 15 white.

Long movies

Everything on the SIM95 network travels in messages of up to 64K, so SimTube cuts every movie into parts of 15K. movie.asp?v=1 answers with a manifest (video/x-simmovie) that lists them:

SIMMOVIE-PARTS 1
title=Dancing Baby
width=160
height=120
fps=10
frames=240
parts=4
part=movie.asp?v=2&p=1
part=movie.asp?v=2&p=2
...

An upload can be up to 60K. Put a longer movie up in pieces: upload the start, then send the rest with Add More Frames on its page. You can do the same on your own disk: SimPlayer opens a manifest file and its parts from the same folder.
